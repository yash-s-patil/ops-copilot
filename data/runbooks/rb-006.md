## Symptoms

- The order was placed with cash on delivery and has stayed in `placed`. It has not been packed.
- The dispatch desk has noted that the automated confirmation call or message was not answered, or that the customer pressed the wrong key.
- The customer contacts support asking why the order has not moved, or says they never received a confirmation call.
- No payment event appears on the order timeline, because nothing has been paid.

## Checks

1. Confirm that the order's payment method is cash on delivery and that the status is `placed`. A prepaid order stuck after payment is rb-004.
2. Check how many confirmation attempts have been made and when. The system tries three times over 24 hours.
3. Check the customer's history. Two or more refused cash-on-delivery parcels in the past six months means new cash-on-delivery orders need a supervisor's approval.
4. Confirm that the phone number on the order is complete. A missing digit is the most common cause.

## Resolution

1. Call the customer on the number on the order and confirm three things: that they placed the order, the delivery address, and that they will pay the stated amount on delivery.
2. If confirmed, mark the order as verified. It is released to the warehouse and continues as a normal order.
3. If the number is wrong and the customer has contacted support through their account, correct the number on the order and then verify.
4. If the customer cannot be reached after two calls on different days, cancel the order under rb-028 and send the standard message explaining why.
5. Offer the customer the option of paying online instead. A prepaid order needs no verification call.

## Compensation

- Nothing has been paid, so there is nothing to refund. Do not request a refund on a cash-on-delivery order that was never delivered.
- Time spent waiting for verification is not a delay under the refund policy, because the order was never confirmed.
- After a verified order is delivered and paid for, later problems such as damage are handled under the normal runbooks. The payments team arranges how a refund on a cash order is paid; support never takes bank details from a customer.

## Escalation

- Escalate to a supervisor before verifying an order for a customer with two or more refused parcels.
- Escalate to L2 if the customer says they did confirm and the system still shows the order as unverified.
- Report repeated failures of the automated call to the dispatch desk lead.
