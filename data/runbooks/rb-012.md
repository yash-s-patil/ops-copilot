## Symptoms

- The order is in `shipped` and the order timeline shows a `carrier_exception` event with no carrier scan after it.
- The exception reason is one of `hub_misroute`, `address_not_found`, `vehicle_breakdown` or `weather_delay`.
- The carrier's public tracking page says "exception" or "held at hub", and the shipment is not moving.
- The customer asks where the parcel is, or what "exception" means.

## Checks

1. Open the order timeline and note the time and reason of the `carrier_exception` event. The delay is counted from that event.
2. Check for a carrier scan after the exception. A later scan means the carrier has recovered the parcel and it is moving again; tell the customer and keep watching.
3. Note the carrier and the tracking reference from the `carrier_pickup` event.
4. Check the carrier notices page. A `weather_delay` or `vehicle_breakdown` affecting many parcels is handled under rb-029.
5. For `address_not_found`, confirm the address with the customer before contacting the carrier.

## Resolution

1. Raise a trace with the carrier, quoting the tracking reference and the hub of the last scan. Carriers answer a trace within 24 hours.
2. Tell the customer that the carrier has reported a problem, that a trace is open, and when you will update them. Do not guess at a delivery date.
3. If the carrier finds the parcel, ask for the new expected delivery date and pass it to the customer.
4. If the parcel is not found within 24 hours, offer the customer an expedited re-shipment under rb-024, and ask the carrier to continue the trace.
5. If the carrier confirms the parcel is lost, a `lost_in_transit_confirmed` event appears on the timeline. Continue under rb-013.

## Compensation

An exception is a delay, not a loss. Until the carrier confirms a loss, only a goodwill refund is possible.

- Premium customers delayed 72 hours or more: offer a 10% goodwill refund.
- Standard customers qualify at 120 hours or more, also at 10%.
- One goodwill refund per order. See rb-021 for the rules and the `refund-policy` document for the caps.
- A full refund needs a confirmed loss. See rb-022.

## Escalation

- Escalate to L2 when a goodwill refund is justified, with the recommended amount and the delay in hours.
- Escalate to L2 when the carrier has not answered the trace within 24 hours.
- An L2 engineer contacts the carrier's account manager when an exception has been open for more than 120 hours.
