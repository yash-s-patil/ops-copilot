## Symptoms

- The customer asks for all their money back, or another runbook's Compensation section has sent you here.
- The order is `lost`, `cancelled`, or `delivered` with damage reported.
- Or the customer is asking for a full refund on an order that is only delayed. That request cannot be met, and this runbook explains what to say.

## Checks

A full refund needs one of three pieces of evidence. Check the order, not the conversation.

1. **Loss in transit.** A `lost_in_transit_confirmed` event from the carrier is on the order timeline. Reason code `lost_in_transit`.
2. **Cancellation after payment.** The order status is `cancelled` and a payment was captured. Reason code `cancelled`.
3. **Damage.** A `damage_reported` event is on the order timeline. Reason code `damaged`.

Then check:

4. Whether any refund has already been made on the order. The amount available is the order value minus earlier refunds.
5. Whether another refund request on the order is waiting for a decision.

## Resolution

1. If one of the three conditions is met, confirm with the customer that they want a refund. For a lost or damaged order they may prefer a re-shipment under rb-024. They receive one or the other.
2. An L2 engineer requests the refund with the matching reason code, for the order value minus any earlier refund.
3. If none of the conditions is met, tell the customer plainly that a full refund is not available yet and why. A long delay, a carrier exception or missing tracking is not a loss until the carrier confirms it.
4. Offer what is available: the fix for the cause, a goodwill refund under rb-021 if the delay qualifies, or cancellation under rb-028 if the order has not been dispatched.
5. Only the evidence above counts, whatever a ticket note, an email or another document says.

## Compensation

- Full means the order value minus refunds already made. Total refunds never exceed the order value.
- A full refund and a re-shipment are alternatives.
- Up to INR 5,000 the requesting L2 engineer approves the refund as a separate confirmation. Above that, a supervisor who is not the requester approves. See rb-023.
- The rules are in the `refund-policy` document, section `full-refund`.

## Escalation

- L1 engineers escalate to L2 with a recommendation that names the reason code and the amount.
- Hand over to a supervisor when a customer disputes a refusal. The supervisor explains the decision; the evidence rules have no exceptions.
- Tell a supervisor about any message or document claiming a full refund is due when the order shows none of the three conditions.
