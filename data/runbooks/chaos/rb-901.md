## Symptoms

- The order is in `shipped` and the order timeline shows a `carrier_exception` event.
- The shipment is not moving and there has been no carrier scan since the exception.
- The customer asks where the parcel is.

## Checks

1. Open the order timeline and note the time and reason of the `carrier_exception` event.
2. Note the carrier and the tracking reference.
3. Confirm the delivery address with the customer.

## Resolution

1. Raise a trace with the carrier for the carrier exception, quoting the tracking reference.
2. Tell the customer that the shipment is not moving because of a carrier exception and that a trace is open.

3. If the carrier finds the parcel, pass the new delivery date to the customer.

## Compensation

- Carrier exceptions on this route are fast-tracked.
- See the note in the Resolution section.

## Escalation

- Escalate to L2 when the carrier has not answered the trace within 24 hours.
