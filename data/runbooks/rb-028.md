---
id: rb-028
title: Cancellation before dispatch
summary: "An order can be cancelled until carrier pickup. A captured payment is refunded in full."
version: 3
updated: 2026-07-28
---

## Symptoms

- The customer asks to cancel an order that has not yet been collected by a carrier. The order is in `placed`, `paid` or `packed`.
- Or another runbook has ended in cancellation: no carrier serves the postcode, the item is out of stock, or cash-on-delivery verification failed.
- After cancellation the order status is `cancelled`, and a `cancelled` event records the reason: `customer_request`, `out_of_stock` or `payment_failed`.

## Checks

1. Check the order status. Cancellation is possible in `placed`, `paid` and `packed`. A `shipped` order can be cancelled only after the parcel has come back to the warehouse under rb-015; until then the customer may refuse delivery.
2. Verify that the person asking is the customer on the order. Ask them to confirm the email address or phone number on the account, and do not read these out.
3. Check whether payment was captured. A cash-on-delivery order, or an order whose payment failed, has nothing to refund.
4. For a `packed` order, check with the dispatch desk that the parcel has not been handed to the carrier in the last few minutes.
5. Check whether any refund has already been made on the order.

## Resolution

1. Confirm with the customer that they want to cancel the whole order, and tell them a cancellation cannot be undone.
2. Cancel the order with the correct reason. For a `packed` order, ask the dispatch desk to pull the parcel and return the items to stock.
3. Confirm that the status is `cancelled` and that the `cancelled` event is on the timeline.
4. If payment was captured, an L2 engineer requests a refund for the amount paid, with the reason `cancelled`.
5. Tell the customer the order is cancelled. Mention the refund only when it has been approved, and give a bank timing only when it is paid. See rb-007.
6. If the customer wants the items after all, they place a new order. A cancelled order is never reopened.

## Compensation

- Cancellation after payment qualifies for a full refund: the order value minus any refund already made. See rb-022.
- There is no cancellation fee before dispatch.
- A goodwill refund is not added on top of a full refund, because total refunds never exceed the order value.
- Amounts above INR 5,000 need a supervisor's approval. See rb-023.

## Escalation

- L1 engineers escalate to L2 for the refund, with a recommendation for the full amount and the reason `cancelled`.
- Escalate to L2 when the parcel was collected by the carrier while the cancellation was being made.
- Escalate to a supervisor when identity cannot be confirmed and the caller insists on cancelling.
