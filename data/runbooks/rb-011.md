## Symptoms

- The warehouse's weight check or an exit scan flags that the parcel does not match the order, and the order is held in `packed`.
- The dispatch desk adds a note such as "weight mismatch" or "label and contents differ".
- The customer usually does not know yet. They may contact support only because dispatch is slow.
- If the customer has already received the wrong item, this runbook does not apply. Use rb-019.

## Checks

1. Confirm the order is still in `packed` and has no `carrier_pickup` event. If it has been collected, ask the carrier to stop it, as described under Resolution.
2. Read the dispatch desk note to see what was found: wrong product, wrong size or colour, or wrong quantity.
3. Check stock for the correct item in the same warehouse.
4. Check whether another order packed at the same time has the mirror-image problem. Two orders are often swapped.

## Resolution

1. Ask the dispatch desk to open the parcel, repack it with the correct item and repeat the weight check.
2. If the correct item is not in stock, follow rb-009.
3. If a swapped order is found, make sure both parcels are corrected, and add a note to both tickets if both customers have tickets.
4. When the repack is confirmed, ask the dispatch desk to book the next carrier collection and tell the customer the dispatch date.
5. If the wrong parcel was collected before it could be stopped, raise a recall with the carrier, quoting the tracking reference. A recall succeeds only before the parcel is out for delivery.
6. If the recall fails, warn the customer that the wrong item is on its way, ask them to refuse delivery if they can, and send the correct item without waiting.

## Compensation

- A repack normally adds less than a day and gives no refund on its own.
- If the repack pushes the time in `packed` past the goodwill threshold for the customer's tier, the delay counts in the usual way. See rb-021.
- If the wrong parcel reaches the customer, they owe nothing for it and are not asked to pay return costs.

## Escalation

- Escalate to L2 when a recall fails and the wrong item is worth more than the correct one.
- Escalate to L2 when a goodwill refund is justified.
- Report the warehouse and the packing station to the dispatch desk lead. Three wrong packs from one station in a week triggers a process review.
