## Symptoms

- The order is in `paid` and has not been packed.
- The order timeline shows a `payment_hold` event, usually with the reason `risk_review`, and no `payment_released` event after it.
- The customer reports that money has left their account but the order has not moved, or that they received no confirmation.
- The warehouse cannot see the order, because held orders are not released to the dispatch desk.

## Checks

1. Confirm on the order timeline that the hold is still open: a `payment_hold` with no later `payment_released`.
2. Note the age of the hold. Most risk reviews clear automatically within 24 hours.
3. Check whether the customer has other recent orders. Several first-time orders of high value in one day are a common trigger and are worth mentioning to the payments team.
4. Check the ticket notes for an earlier verification request, so the customer is not asked twice.

## Resolution

1. If the hold is less than 24 hours old, tell the customer the payment is being verified and that the order will move within a day. No other action is needed.
2. If the hold is older than 24 hours, ask the payments team to run the standard verification check on the order. Give them the order number only.
3. If payments asks for confirmation from the customer, contact the customer and ask them to confirm the order amount and the last four digits of the card or the UPI handle used. Never ask for a full card number, a PIN or a one-time password.
4. When verification passes, the payments team releases the hold. Confirm that `payment_released` appears on the timeline and that the order is visible to the warehouse.
5. If verification fails, the payments team cancels the order and the captured amount is returned automatically.

## Compensation

- No compensation unless the delay passes the goodwill threshold for the customer's tier: 72 hours for premium customers and 120 hours for standard customers. Below the threshold, say plainly that the order is not eligible for a refund yet and give the reason.
- A hold is a delay like any other once the threshold is reached. See rb-021.
- A customer asking where a refund for a cancelled order is should be handled under rb-007.

## Escalation

- Escalate to L2 when the hold is older than 48 hours and the payments team has not responded.
- Escalate to L2 when a goodwill refund is justified, with the recommended amount.
- Never release a hold yourself or ask the warehouse to pack a held order. Only the payments team can release a payment.
