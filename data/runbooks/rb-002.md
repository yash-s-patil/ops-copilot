## Symptoms

- The order is in `packed` and no carrier pickup is booked.
- The order timeline shows `address_validation_failed` with the reason `service_unavailable`, or the dispatch desk has noted that no carrier accepts the postcode.
- The address itself is correct. The customer confirms the postcode, and it matches the locality.
- This is most common for remote postcodes and for areas a carrier has suspended because of flooding or road closures.

## Checks

1. Confirm on the order timeline that the failure reason is `service_unavailable` and not `postcode_mismatch`. A mismatch is rb-001.
2. Look up the postcode in the carrier coverage list for all four carriers: BlueArrow Logistics, Sahyadri Express, Deccan Couriers and Konkan Cargo. Coverage differs between them.
3. Check the carrier notices page for a temporary suspension in the area. A suspension has an expected end date; a coverage gap does not.
4. Note how long the order has been in `packed`.

## Resolution

1. If another carrier covers the postcode, ask the dispatch desk to reassign the order to that carrier and book a pickup. Tell the customer the new carrier and dispatch date.
2. If the gap is a temporary suspension of less than three days, tell the customer the expected date and keep the ticket open until pickup.
3. If no carrier covers the postcode, offer the customer two options: an alternate delivery address, such as a workplace or a relative in a serviceable area, or collection from the nearest carrier pickup point.
4. If the customer accepts neither, cancel the order with their agreement. Use rb-028 for the cancellation.

## Compensation

- If the order is cancelled after payment, the customer receives a full refund under the reason `cancelled`. See rb-022.
- If the order is delivered to an alternate address, the time it spent stuck counts as delay. A goodwill refund is possible once the threshold for the customer's tier is reached. See rb-021.
- Meridian accepted the order for a postcode it could not serve, so do not treat this as a delay caused by the customer.

## Escalation

- Escalate to L2 for any refund. L1 engineers record a recommendation with the amount and reason.
- Report the postcode to the dispatch desk lead so the checkout coverage list can be corrected. Add the postcode to the ticket note.
- If more than five orders for the same postcode are stuck on one day, tell a supervisor: it may be an unannounced carrier suspension, which is handled under rb-029.
