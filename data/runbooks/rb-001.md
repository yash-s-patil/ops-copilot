## Symptoms

- The order is in `packed` and has not been collected by a carrier.
- The order timeline shows one or more `address_validation_failed` events after the `packed` event. The reason is usually `postcode_mismatch`: the postcode does not belong to the city or locality in the address.
- Validation is retried automatically every few hours, so the same failure often appears several times.
- The customer reports that the order "has not moved" or is "still showing as packed".

## Checks

1. Open the order timeline and confirm the most recent event is `address_validation_failed`. If there is no such event, this is not an address problem; use rb-008.
2. Read the failure reason. `postcode_mismatch` needs the customer; a reason of `service_unavailable` is rb-002.
3. Note when the order entered `packed`. The delay is counted from that moment, not from the first failed validation.
4. Read the ticket notes to see whether the customer has already confirmed or corrected the address.

## Resolution

1. Contact the customer and read back the full delivery address, including the postcode. Ask them to confirm or correct it.
2. Update the delivery address on the order with exactly what the customer confirmed.
3. Re-trigger address validation from the order page. A successful validation releases the order to the dispatch desk, and a carrier pickup is booked in the next collection slot.
4. Tell the customer the address is fixed and give the dispatch date only after the pickup is booked.
5. If validation fails again with the corrected address, ask the dispatch desk to validate the address manually with the carrier.

## Compensation

The delay counts toward a goodwill refund, because the failure was not caused by the customer unless they entered a wrong postcode and then did not respond.

- Premium customers delayed 72 hours or more: offer a 10% goodwill refund.
- Standard customers qualify at 120 hours or more, also at 10%.
- One goodwill refund per order. See rb-021 for the full rules and the `refund-policy` document, section `goodwill`, for the caps.

L1 engineers cannot refund. Record the amount as a recommendation when escalating.

## Escalation

- Escalate to L2 when a goodwill refund is justified, with the recommended amount and the delay in hours.
- Escalate to L2 when validation still fails after a manual check by the dispatch desk.
- If the customer cannot be reached after two attempts on different days, set the ticket to pending customer and note both attempts. Do not cancel the order without the customer's agreement.
