## Symptoms

- The order is `delivered`, and the customer reports that the box was crushed, wet, torn or opened, or that the item inside is broken or does not work.
- Once the report is recorded, the order timeline shows a `damage_reported` event with a detail such as `box_crushed`, `item_broken` or `water_damage`.
- The customer usually offers photos.
- An intact box with the wrong contents is rb-019. An intact box with items missing is rb-010.

## Checks

1. Ask the customer for photos of the outer box, the shipping label and the damaged item. Ask them to keep all packaging.
2. Check the delivery date. Damage must be reported within seven days of delivery.
3. Look for a `damage_reported` event on the order timeline. The returns desk logs it after reviewing the customer's photos. If it is missing, send the photos to the returns desk and wait for the event.
4. Check whether a refund has already been made on the order.
5. Check stock for a replacement before offering one.

## Resolution

1. Apologise and confirm that the damage has been recorded. The photos are enough; the customer need not explain how it happened.
2. Offer the choice: a full refund or a replacement. The customer receives one or the other.
3. Book a return pickup for the damaged item under rb-020, unless the item is unsafe to transport or worth less than INR 500, in which case the customer may dispose of it.
4. For a replacement, ask the dispatch desk to send it without waiting for the return, by the fastest carrier on the route.
5. For a refund, an L2 engineer requests it with the reason `damaged`. The amount is the order value minus any refund already made.
6. The dispatch desk files the carrier claim, using the customer's photos.

## Compensation

- Reported damage qualifies for a full refund. See rb-022 and the `refund-policy` document, section `full-refund`.
- A refund above INR 5,000 needs a supervisor who is not the requester. See rb-023.
- When only one item of several is damaged, the refund is still requested for the order, so offer a replacement of that item first.
- A goodwill refund for delay is not available on a delivered order.

## Escalation

- L1 engineers escalate to L2 with a recommendation for the full amount and the reason `damaged`.
- Escalate to L2 when the damage is reported more than seven days after delivery, or when the photos do not show the damage described.
- Escalate to a supervisor when a customer reports damage on three or more orders in six months.
