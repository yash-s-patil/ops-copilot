## Symptoms

- The original parcel is stuck, missing or lost, and the customer would rather have the goods than a refund.
- Another runbook has sent you here: a carrier trace found nothing within 24 hours, a scanner fault is holding the parcel at a hub, or the carrier has confirmed a loss.
- For a premium customer, the order is stuck in packed or stuck in transit and stock is available.

## Checks

1. Check that the customer qualifies. Any customer qualifies after an unsuccessful trace or a confirmed loss. A premium customer also qualifies when the order is stuck as defined in the `shipping-sla` document.
2. Check stock in the region's warehouses. Without stock there is nothing to offer; see rb-009.
3. Confirm the delivery address and phone number with the customer. A re-shipment to a wrong address repeats the problem.
4. Check whether the original parcel is still in transit. If it is, it must be recalled.
5. Check that no full refund has been requested or paid on the order.

## Resolution

1. Offer the re-shipment and explain it: a replacement order, sent by the fastest carrier on the route, at no charge.
2. If the customer accepts, record their agreement in a ticket note.
3. Ask the carrier to recall the original parcel, quoting the tracking reference. If it is later delivered anyway, ask the customer to refuse it or arrange a return pickup under rb-020.
4. Ask the dispatch desk to create the replacement and book it with the fastest carrier, usually BlueArrow Logistics.
5. Give the customer the new tracking reference and the delivery date once the pickup is confirmed.
6. Keep the ticket open until the replacement is delivered.

## Compensation

- A re-shipment and a full refund are alternatives. A customer who accepts a replacement for a lost or damaged order does not also receive a full refund.
- A goodwill refund for the delay may be given as well, when the original order is still undelivered, qualifies under rb-021 and has had none. It is not available once a loss is confirmed.
- The customer pays nothing for the replacement or for its delivery.
- If the replacement is also lost or damaged, the customer is offered a full refund and is not asked to accept another re-shipment.

## Escalation

- An L1 engineer may offer and arrange a re-shipment for orders up to INR 10,000. Above that, escalate to L2.
- Escalate to L2 when both the original and the replacement have been delivered and the customer wishes to keep both.
- Escalate to a supervisor when a customer has received three re-shipments in six months.
