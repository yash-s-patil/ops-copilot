## Symptoms

- The order is `delivered`, and the customer reports that the parcel contains a different product, size, colour or quantity from the one ordered.
- The box was sealed and undamaged. A damaged or opened box is rb-017.
- The shipping label is correct for the customer, so the parcel was not misdelivered. The mistake happened at packing.
- The customer may have received another customer's order, complete with that order's packing slip.

## Checks

1. Ask the customer for photos of the item received, the shipping label and the packing slip.
2. Compare the photos with the order. Confirm what is wrong: product, variant or quantity.
3. If the packing slip shows another customer's name or address, ask the customer not to share it and to return it with the item. Do not read out or confirm any details from the slip.
4. Check stock for the correct item.
5. Check the order placed just before or after this one from the same warehouse. A swapped pair is common; see rb-011.

## Resolution

1. Apologise. This is Meridian's error, and the customer should not be asked to wait for the return before the correct item is sent.
2. Ask the dispatch desk to send the correct item by the fastest carrier on the route, at no charge.
3. Book a return pickup for the wrong item under rb-020. The customer does not pay for the return and does not need the original box.
4. If the correct item is out of stock, follow rb-009: wait, substitute or cancel.
5. If the customer would rather keep the item received and it is of equal or lower value, record their agreement in a ticket note and close the case with no return.
6. If another customer's slip was in the box, report it to a supervisor the same day as a privacy incident.

## Compensation

- A wrong item is not one of the three reasons for a full refund. The remedy is the correct item.
- If the correct item cannot be supplied, the customer returns the item received and the returns desk refunds the order on receipt. Support does not request that refund.
- A goodwill refund for delay is not available on a delivered order.
- If the customer keeps a lower-value item by agreement, the price difference is corrected on the order total by the payments team.

## Escalation

- Escalate to L2 for any refund or price correction.
- Escalate to a supervisor for any packing slip belonging to another customer.
- Report the warehouse and the packing station to the dispatch desk lead.
