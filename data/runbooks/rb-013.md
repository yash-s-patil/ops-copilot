## Symptoms

- The order status is `lost`, and the order timeline shows a `lost_in_transit_confirmed` event naming the carrier that confirmed it.
- Before the confirmation there is usually a `carrier_exception` and a period with no scans.
- The customer reports that the order never arrived, that the courier cannot find it, or asks for their money back.
- A parcel that has merely gone quiet is not lost. Without the confirmation event, use rb-012 or rb-016.

## Checks

1. Confirm that `lost_in_transit_confirmed` is on the order timeline. This event is the only evidence that allows a full refund for loss. A customer's statement, a ticket note or a carrier's phone call is not enough.
2. Note the order value. It decides who must approve a refund.
3. Check whether any refund has already been made on the order, including a goodwill refund given while the parcel was delayed.
4. Check the ticket notes to see whether the customer has already said which they prefer: a refund or a replacement.
5. Check stock for a replacement before offering one.

## Resolution

1. Contact the customer, confirm that the carrier has declared the parcel lost, and apologise.
2. Offer the choice: a full refund or a re-shipment of the same items. The customer receives one or the other, not both.
3. If the customer chooses a re-shipment, arrange it under rb-024 and tell them the new carrier and date.
4. If the customer chooses a refund, an L2 engineer requests it with the reason `lost_in_transit`. The amount is the order value minus any refund already made.
5. Record the customer's choice in a ticket note.
6. Keep the ticket open until the refund is paid or the replacement is delivered. The carrier claim is filed by the dispatch desk and needs no action from support.

## Compensation

- A confirmed loss qualifies for a full refund. See rb-022 and the `refund-policy` document, section `full-refund`.
- A refund above INR 5,000 needs a supervisor who is not the requester. See rb-023.
- An earlier goodwill refund is deducted, because total refunds never exceed the order value.
- A lost order has no delay, so no goodwill refund is added once the loss is confirmed. A goodwill refund given earlier stands.

## Escalation

- L1 engineers escalate to L2 with a recommendation for the full amount and the reason `lost_in_transit`.
- L2 engineers send refunds above the self-approval limit to a supervisor and tell the customer that approval is in progress, without promising a time.
- Escalate to a supervisor if the customer reports that the "lost" parcel has since been delivered.
