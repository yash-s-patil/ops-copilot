## Symptoms

- An order is delayed and has not been delivered: stuck in `paid`, stuck in `packed`, or `shipped` with a carrier exception or no new scan.
- The customer asks for compensation, or the delay has passed the point where Meridian offers it without being asked.
- Another runbook has sent you here from its Compensation section.

## Checks

1. Establish the customer's tier, premium or standard, from the customer record.
2. Work out the delay in whole hours, as defined in the `shipping-sla` document, section `stuck-definitions`. For `paid` and `packed` orders it runs from the moment the order entered that status. For `shipped` orders it runs from the carrier exception, or from the last new scan.
3. Compare the delay with the threshold: 72 hours for premium customers, 120 hours for standard customers.
4. Check that no goodwill refund has already been made on the order.
5. Check that the delay was not caused by the customer, for example missed delivery attempts or an address change after packing.

## Resolution

1. If the order qualifies, work out the amount. The standard offer is 10% of the order value for both tiers.
2. An L2 engineer requests the refund with the reason `goodwill_delay` and states the delay in hours in the reason text.
3. An L1 engineer cannot request a refund. Escalate the ticket to L2 and record the amount and reason as a recommendation.
4. If the order does not qualify yet, tell the customer so, and say how many hours of delay are needed for their tier. Do not promise a refund in advance.
5. Fix the cause of the delay as well, using the runbook for that cause. A refund does not close the ticket.

## Compensation

| Customer tier | Minimum delay | Maximum refund |
|---|---|---|
| Premium | 72 hours | 15% of order value |
| Standard | 120 hours | 10% of order value |

- Going above 10% for a premium customer needs grounds stated in the reason text, and never exceeds the 15% cap.
- One goodwill refund per order, however long the delay lasts.
- A request above the cap is refused. Correct the amount instead of asking again with a different reason.
- Once the order is delivered, a goodwill refund for the earlier delay is no longer available.

## Escalation

- L1 to L2: every case where a goodwill refund is justified.
- Amounts up to INR 5,000 are approved by the requesting L2 engineer as a separate confirmation. Larger amounts need a supervisor; see rb-023.
- Hand over to a supervisor when a customer disputes the cap. The supervisor explains it; nobody can approve more than the cap.
