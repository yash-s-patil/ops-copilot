## Symptoms

- The carrier's tracking page shows "return to origin" or "RTO initiated", and carrier scans on the order timeline move back toward the warehouse.
- The order is still in `shipped`. It does not change status until the warehouse receives the parcel.
- The cause is three failed delivery attempts, a refused delivery, or an address the carrier could not find.
- The customer may not know. Some contact support expecting delivery, others because they refused the parcel on purpose.

## Checks

1. Find out why the parcel was returned. The carrier's tracking page gives a reason code; ask the carrier if it does not.
2. Check whether the customer refused the delivery. A refusal after a failed redirect or a wrong-item warning is expected; see rb-003 and rb-011.
3. Confirm the delivery address and phone number with the customer.
4. Check where the parcel is now. Once a return has started, most carriers will not turn it around.
5. Note whether the order was prepaid or cash on delivery.

## Resolution

1. Tell the customer the parcel is coming back to the warehouse and why.
2. Ask what they want when it arrives: re-dispatch to the same or a corrected address, or cancellation.
3. Wait for the warehouse to confirm receipt and inspect the parcel. Do not re-dispatch or cancel before receipt is confirmed.
4. For a re-dispatch, ask the dispatch desk to book a new pickup, and give the customer the new tracking reference. The first re-dispatch is free.
5. For a cancellation, cancel the order under rb-028 after receipt.
6. If the parcel does not reach the warehouse within the carrier's return time, which is the same as its delivery commitment, raise a trace under rb-012.

## Compensation

- A prepaid order cancelled after return is refunded in full under the reason `cancelled`. See rb-022.
- A cash-on-delivery order has no payment to return.
- Delay caused by failed attempts or a refusal by the customer does not count toward a goodwill refund.
- If the return was the carrier's error, for example a wrong address label, the full delay counts. See rb-021.

## Escalation

- Escalate to L2 for any refund, and when the returned parcel arrives damaged or incomplete.
- Escalate to L2 when the parcel is lost on the return leg. Until the carrier confirms the loss, no full refund for loss is possible.
- Tell a supervisor when a customer has had three orders returned to origin in six months.
