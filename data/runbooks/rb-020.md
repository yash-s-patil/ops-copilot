## Symptoms

- A return pickup was booked for a damaged, wrong or unwanted item, and the carrier did not collect it.
- The customer reports that nobody came, or that the rider arrived without a label or refused the parcel.
- The carrier's tracking page for the return shows "pickup attempted", "customer unavailable" or no activity at all.
- A refund or replacement that depends on the return is waiting.

## Checks

1. Find the return booking and its tracking reference in the ticket notes.
2. Read the carrier's reason for the failed pickup on the tracking page.
3. Confirm the pickup address and phone number with the customer. A return is collected from the address the customer names, which is not always the delivery address.
4. Ask the customer whether the item is packed and ready. Riders do not wait while an item is packed.
5. Count the failed attempts and note which side missed each one.

## Resolution

1. Rebook the pickup with the same carrier for a day the customer chooses, and add the customer's phone number and a landmark to the instructions.
2. Send the customer the return label again and ask them to attach it before the rider arrives.
3. If the carrier has failed twice, book the next pickup with a different carrier.
4. If pickups keep failing, offer the customer the nearest carrier drop-off point. Give them the label and tell them to keep the receipt.
5. Where the outcome does not need the item back, do not make the customer wait for the return. A replacement for a damaged or wrong item is sent at once under rb-017 and rb-019.
6. When the return is scanned by the carrier, update the ticket with the return tracking reference.

## Compensation

- A failed return pickup does not change what the customer is owed. The refund or replacement decided under the original runbook stands.
- A full refund for reported damage or a confirmed loss does not wait for the return. A refund for goods the customer no longer wants is paid by the returns desk once the item is received.
- There is no goodwill refund for a failed return pickup, because the order has been delivered.
- The customer never pays for a return that results from Meridian's or a carrier's error.

## Escalation

- Escalate to L2 after three failed pickups, whoever missed them.
- Escalate to L2 when the customer says the item was collected but the carrier shows no scan. The L2 engineer raises a trace on the return.
- Tell a supervisor before writing off a return for an item worth more than INR 5,000.
