## Symptoms

- The order is in `shipped` and there has been no new `carrier_scan` event for 72 hours or more.
- There is no `carrier_exception` event. If there is one, use rb-012.
- Alternatively, the order timeline shows a very large number of scans at the same hub with the same scan type, minutes apart. The tracking page repeats the same line.
- The customer reports that tracking has not changed for days, or asks whether the parcel is lost.

## Checks

1. Open the order timeline and find the last new scan. A scan that repeats the hub and scan type of the one before it is not a new scan and does not show movement.
2. Note the hub of the last new scan and how long ago it was. The delay is counted from that scan.
3. If there are hundreds of identical scans, do not read through them. Repeated identical scans usually mean a scanner fault at the hub: the parcel is sitting on a conveyor or shelf and being read over and over.
4. Check the carrier notices page for a known problem at that hub.
5. Compare the time since pickup with the carrier's delivery commitment in the `shipping-sla` document.

## Resolution

1. Ask the carrier for a hub exception report, quoting the tracking reference and the hub code, for example PNQ-2. The report states whether the parcel is physically at the hub.
2. Tell the customer that the carrier has been asked to locate the parcel and when you will update them.
3. If the parcel is found at the hub, ask the carrier to move it on the next departure and to confirm the new delivery date.
4. If the carrier reports a scanner fault, ask them to raise a hub exception so the parcel is handled by hand, and offer the customer an expedited re-shipment under rb-024.
5. If the carrier cannot locate the parcel within 24 hours, raise a full trace under rb-012.

## Compensation

Stale tracking is a delay, not a loss.

- A goodwill refund is possible once the delay reaches 72 hours for premium customers or 120 hours for standard customers. The standard offer is 10% of the order value.
- One goodwill refund per order. See rb-021.
- A full refund needs a `lost_in_transit_confirmed` event. See rb-022.

## Escalation

- Escalate to L2 when a goodwill refund is justified, with the recommended amount and the delay in hours.
- Escalate to L2 when the carrier cannot confirm the parcel's location within 24 hours.
- Report a scanner fault that affects more than one order to a supervisor. See rb-029.
