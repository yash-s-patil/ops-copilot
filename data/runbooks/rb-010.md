## Symptoms

- The customer received a parcel with fewer items than they ordered, and the box was sealed and undamaged.
- The packing slip in the box lists only the items that were sent, or is marked "1 of 2".
- The order shows as `delivered`, although part of it has not arrived.
- Sometimes the order is still `shipped` and the customer is warned in advance by a dispatch message that mentions two parcels.

## Checks

1. Ask the customer which items arrived and which are missing. Ask for a photo of the packing slip.
2. Ask the dispatch desk whether the order was split on purpose. Orders are split when one item is oversized or is stocked in another warehouse.
3. If it was split, find the second parcel's tracking reference and its status.
4. If it was not split, ask the dispatch desk to check the packing record for the order. A short pick means the missing item never left the warehouse.
5. If the box was opened or resealed, this is not a partial shipment. Use rb-017.

## Resolution

1. For a planned split, give the customer the second tracking reference and the expected date. Apologise that the dispatch message was unclear.
2. For a short pick with stock available, ask the dispatch desk to send the missing items as a second parcel at no charge, by the fastest carrier on the route.
3. For a short pick without stock, follow rb-009 for the missing items only: wait, substitute or cancel those items.
4. Keep the ticket open until every item is delivered or cancelled.
5. If the second parcel then stalls, handle it under the carrier runbooks as its own shipment.

## Compensation

- Items that are cancelled because they cannot be supplied are returned to the customer by the payments team as a correction to the order total. The remaining order value is the basis for any later refund.
- A goodwill refund for delay applies only if the order as a whole is still undelivered and past the threshold for the customer's tier. See rb-021.
- A full refund is not available for a partial shipment. A customer who no longer wants the delivered items returns them, and the returns desk refunds them on receipt.

## Escalation

- Escalate to L2 when the customer wants to send back the items already delivered, because a return pickup must be arranged. See rb-020.
- Escalate to L2 when the warehouse record says the item was packed but the customer says it was not in a sealed box.
- Report repeated short picks from one warehouse to the dispatch desk lead.
