## Symptoms

- The order has been in `packed` for more than 48 hours. The shipping commitment is carrier pickup within 48 hours of packing.
- There is no `carrier_pickup` event on the order timeline.
- The customer reports the order as "packed but not dispatched", or says the app has shown the same status for days.

## Checks

1. Open the order timeline and note when the `packed` event happened. The delay is counted from that moment.
2. Look for a blocking event after `packed`. An `address_validation_failed` event means the order cannot be handed to a carrier: follow rb-001 for a postcode mismatch, or rb-002 if the postcode is not serviceable.
3. If there is no blocking event, check whether a pickup was ever booked. An order with no booking has usually been missed at the dispatch desk; an order with a booking that passed means the carrier did not collect.
4. Check the carrier notices page for an outage affecting the warehouse. If many orders from one warehouse are stuck, see rb-029.

## Resolution

1. With no blocking event and no booking, ask the dispatch desk at the warehouse named on the `packed` event to book a carrier pickup in the next collection slot.
2. If a booked pickup was missed, ask the dispatch desk to rebook, and to use a different carrier if the same carrier has missed two collections.
3. Confirm the booking exists, then tell the customer the new dispatch date. Do not give a date before the booking is confirmed.
4. Check the order the next working day. When `carrier_pickup` appears, tell the customer the carrier and the tracking reference.
5. If the parcel cannot be found in the warehouse, treat it as out of stock under rb-009.

## Compensation

Time in `packed` counts as delay from the `packed` event.

- Premium customers delayed 72 hours or more, and standard customers delayed 120 hours or more, qualify for a goodwill refund. The standard offer is 10% of the order value.
- One goodwill refund per order. See rb-021.
- A premium customer may also be offered an expedited re-shipment when stock is available. See rb-024.

## Escalation

- Escalate to L2 when a goodwill refund is justified. Record the recommended amount and the delay in hours.
- Escalate to L2 when the order has been packed for more than 96 hours, or when the warehouse has not booked a pickup after two requests.
- A premium customer's ticket that is more than 48 hours old moves to priority handling under rb-025.
