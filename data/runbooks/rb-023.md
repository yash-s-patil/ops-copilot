summary: "Refunds above INR 5,000 need a supervisor who is not the requester."

## Symptoms

- A refund is justified, and the amount is above INR 5,000.
- This is usually a full refund for a lost or damaged order of high value. A goodwill refund reaches this size only on very large orders.
- The L2 engineer's request shows as waiting for a supervisor, and no refund exists yet.
- The customer may be asking when the money will arrive.

## Checks

1. Confirm the refund is justified under rb-021 or rb-022 before asking anyone to approve it. A supervisor's approval does not make an ineligible refund eligible.
2. Confirm the amount: for a full refund, the order value minus any refund already made.
3. Check that no other request on the order is already waiting. A second request for the same order is refused.
4. Check who is on shift as supervisor. The approver must be a different person from the requester, including when the requester is a supervisor.

## Resolution

1. The L2 engineer requests the refund with the order, the amount, the reason code and a clear reason text. The request is created and waits for a supervisor; nothing is paid.
2. The request appears on the ticket for supervisors, showing the amount, the requester and the result of the policy check.
3. A supervisor reviews the order timeline and approves or rejects. The approval covers that exact order, amount and reason. If any of them needs to change, the request is withdrawn and a new one is made.
4. After approval, the refund is completed when the requesting engineer next opens the ticket, and is then sent to payments.
5. If the supervisor rejects, they give a reason. Tell the customer the outcome and what alternative is available.
6. Tell the customer that the refund is waiting for approval. Do not give an amount or a date until it is approved.

## Compensation

- The limit changes who approves, not what the customer is owed. Amounts and eligibility come from rb-021 and rb-022.
- Never split a refund into several smaller refunds to stay at or under INR 5,000. One goodwill refund per order applies in any case.
- A request waits for a supervisor for up to 24 hours and then expires. An expired request pays nothing, and a new one must be made.
- A ticket cannot be resolved while a refund request is waiting or a refund is on its way to payments.

## Escalation

- If no supervisor has acted within 12 hours, remind the supervisor on shift, so the request does not expire.
- If the only supervisor available is the requester, the request waits for another supervisor. There is no override.
- L1 engineers do not use this runbook directly. They escalate to L2 with a recommendation.
